Why does Srilanka beautiful?

Sri Lanka, officially the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon), is a South Asian island nation in the Indian Ocean with a population of approximately 21.9 million. Known for its rich 3,000-year history, it is a republic with a parliamentary system, featuring Colombo as its commercial capital and Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte as the legislative capital.

Sri Lanka’s transport network relies heavily on roads (93% of land transport), featuring an extensive bus system, scenic rail routes, and popular three-wheeler (tuk-tuk) taxis.

Volleyball is officially recognized as the national sport of Sri Lanka, designated in 1991. While cricket is by far the most popular and celebrated sport in the country, volleyball holds the official title and is widely played across the island. It was introduced to Sri Lanka in 1916.
